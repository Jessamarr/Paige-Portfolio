About me

LinkedIn ↗

I am a recent graduate of the University of Waterloo with a Bachelor of Environmental Studies in Planning, specializing in Urban Design and minoring in Geography and Environmental Management. Throughout my degree, I competed on the varsity cheer team, volunteered as an orientation leader, and participated in the Planning Students’ Association. I graduated with distinction and developed a strong interest in urban design and community development.

Through planning and marketing co-op experiences, I developed skills in research, community engagement, communications, graphic design, and project coordination. I enjoy combining creativity with practical problem-solving to develop ideas that make a positive impact on people and the places they live.

02Year 2University of Waterloo campus

Eunoia
connection

Design process

Concept

For this assignment, I was tasked with reimagining a site on the University of Waterloo’s campus, integrating elements of playful interaction and sustainable design principles.

The big idea

Students often balance demanding academic workloads with personal responsibilities, which can take a toll on their physical and mental well-being. Guided by my Big Idea, Eunoia Connection, I wanted to design a space that fosters mental wellness, encourages connection, and provides students with an opportunity to recharge and have fun.

03Year 3Waterloo

The urban
backyard

Concept

Backyards are safe and comfortable places to relax, play, and socialize. However, they are a privilege not everyone can enjoy. The Urban Backyard is a transformative public space designed to provide these cherished experiences to all members of the community. Imagine a space where conversation pits with fireplaces invite year-round gatherings, fostering warmth and connection even on the coldest days.

04Year 4Land-based learning

Kiinomaage-aki

The vision

The concept for Kiinomaage-aki (Land of Knowledge) is to create a site that tells the story of Indigenous culture and traditions through land-based learning, where every space offers an opportunity for education and engagement. This approach transforms the site into a living classroom, ensuring that knowledge is not just presented but actively experienced.
